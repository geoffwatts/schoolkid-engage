# Schoolkid Engage support

## Get help or report a problem

[Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/geoffwatts/schoolkid-engage/issues/new). You'll need a free GitHub
account. You can also [browse existing issues](https://github.com/geoffwatts/schoolkid-engage/issues) to see if
someone has already asked.

**Issues are public.** Please don't include your child's name, your school, your sign-in details or screenshots
of real school data. Describe what happened and what you expected instead; use the demo to show a screen if that
helps.

Useful details to include: your iPhone or iPad model, the iOS version, and the app version (shown in the App Store
or in TestFlight).

## Things to try first

- **The app says my sign-in has expired.** Tap **Sign in again** and complete your school's login. Schools end
  sessions from time to time.
- **I can't sign in at all.** Check your school uses SEQTA Engage for parents and that you can sign in on the
  school's own website. If your school offers a "Connect mobile app" link, try **Setup link** on the sign-in screen.
- **Something looks out of date.** Pull down on any list to refresh it.
- **No notifications.** Check **Notify me about assessments** is on (account button, top left) and that
  notifications and Background App Refresh are allowed for Schoolkid Engage in Settings. iOS decides when
  background checks run, so alerts can take a few hours.
- **Want to look around first?** On the welcome screen, tap **Explore the demo** to use the app with fictional data.

## Privacy

Schoolkid Engage talks only to your school and keeps your sign-in on your device. See the
[privacy policy](docs/PRIVACY.md).

Schoolkid Engage is an independent app. It is not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by SEQTA,
Education Horizons or any school, so they can't help with the app, and we can't help with your school account.
