# Schoolkid Engage privacy

Schoolkid Engage connects directly to the school portal you choose. It has no developer-operated server, advertising, analytics, or cloud AI service. School and identity-provider services receive the requests needed for authentication and reading school content.

Passwords and mobile setup tokens are used during authentication and are not saved. Saved account metadata and session cookies are kept in the device Keychain, accessible only while the device is unlocked and restricted to that device. Removing an account deletes its saved local session; it does not revoke sessions on other devices.

School sign-in pages open in a browser that belongs to that one school account. Its website data (the school's and its identity provider's sign-in cookies) stays on the device, in app storage that isn't shared with Safari, other apps or the app's other accounts. Keeping it lets a lunch-order site that uses the same school sign-in connect, and sign in again when its own session ends, without asking for a password each time. Removing the account deletes this browser data; a sign-in that is cancelled before it finishes has its browser data deleted too.

The app displays personal information supplied by the school, including names, assessments, reports, timetable and attachments. Reading caches are held in memory for five minutes. Public school names are saved as local preferences. To announce new assessment updates (other bell-feed events are not announced), the app saves the IDs of bell-feed events it has already seen (not their content) as local preferences, and iOS background app refresh reads each saved account's feed directly from the school; notifications are created on the device, with no push server. For the unread badges it also saves the IDs of updates the Updates screen has shown, and a count of those not yet seen. For News and Reports unread indicators, the app also saves item identities and which have been seen as local preferences, scoped to the school account (and child for reports). A notice is marked seen when it's opened; notices listed for days before the account began tracking (its first sign-in, or the first launch of a version with this) count as seen. Report badges count only reports dated after the account’s local tracking baseline, saved when tracking starts; older and undated reports do not appear as unread. Opening a report clears its unread marker. These identities are deleted when that account is removed. Notice text and report contents are not saved with this state. Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets read a snapshot of the selected child's lessons and cycle days for about the next two weeks, kept in a container shared only between the app and its widgets on this device; it is cleared when the account is removed. Reminder notes, their colours and the lesson names or kinds of lesson they follow are saved as local preferences, and the widget snapshot includes each day's reminders. If the morning alert is switched on, each school day's reminders for the next two weeks are scheduled as local notifications on the device. To offer every child in the child switcher, the app asks each saved account's school for its children when it starts; those names are held in memory only. Office previews and explicit attachment sharing use temporary local files; the app removes those files when the preview or share operation finishes. Files the user exports to another app are controlled by that destination.

If you connect your school's My Student Account lunch-order site, the app keeps that site's session cookies in the Keychain, linked to the school account, and reads account names, balances, menus, orders and account activity directly from the school's site; these are held in memory. The selected child’s last loaded lunch balance is also saved in the local shared widget snapshot for display in Home Screen widgets. The app sends an order only when you place one, paid from the student's account balance. It never sees or stores card details: topping up opens the site's own page, which sends card details to the site. Removing the school account removes the saved session and the account's browser, with the site's data.

Subject classification uses local rules and, when available, Apple's on-device model. It does not send school content to an external AI provider.

Debug simulator builds additionally save an owner-only local authentication file to preserve sign-in across unsigned development rebuilds. That fallback and response-shape diagnostics are excluded from release builds. No real student records or session credentials are included as app resources or test fixtures.

This document describes the app implementation; school and identity-provider privacy policies apply to their services.

Questions about privacy: see [support](https://github.com/geoffwatts/schoolkid-engage/blob/main/SUPPORT.md). Issues are public, so please don't include personal or school details.
